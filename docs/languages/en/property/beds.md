# Physical beds and bunk beds

A physical bed describes the furniture and its capacity. A separately reservable bed space is a rental unit. Selecting a bed in the amenities or inventory catalog does not create a physical bed space that can be reserved.

## Define a bed

1. Open Physical Bed Definition from bed management within floor and room definitions.
2. Optionally choose a single bed, double bed or a bunk with 2, 3 or 4 levels as a preset.
3. Check the bed code, room and floor.
4. Adjust the bed type, physical structure, number of levels and dimensions.
5. Check each space's code, position and guest capacity.
6. Review Active and Separately reservable together with the room's rental mode.
7. Select Save bed.

Selecting a preset only fills the fields. You must save to create the record.

## Bunk-bed example

A two-level bunk groups two physical bed spaces. Track lower and upper levels with separate codes. A double bed may be one bed space with capacity for two people. Bed-space count and guest capacity are not the same measure.

## Add beds from the room form

The room create/edit form lets you choose bed types and quantities. These are added to existing physical beds. To change an existing bed, edit its record rather than adding another one.

After saving, check bed codes in the definitions list and the layout in [3D View](three-dimensional-view.md). If a bed is missing from the sales board, check the room's rental mode and whether the bed can be reserved separately.

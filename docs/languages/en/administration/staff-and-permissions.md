# Staff, permissions and audit history

Open Staff Definitions and System Logs.

## Set up a staff account

1. Open Staff Definitions using an authorized account.
2. Create a staff member or edit an existing person.
3. Complete identity, login and displayed staff information.
4. Assign the role and property access appropriate for the job.
5. Save and verify that the employee can sign in to the correct business with their own card.

Do not share one account among multiple employees. Personal accounts identify who performed each operation and keep preferences separate.

## A menu or action is missing

Check the employee's role and property access. Changing the role alone may not grant access to another property. Updating or deleting a staff account may terminate open sessions, requiring the person to sign in again.

## Find a change in System Logs

1. Open System Logs.
2. Filter by the relevant period, operation or record using the available controls.
3. Inspect the operation summary, room/property and staff member.
4. Compare before-and-after values and read any cancellation or renovation reason.

Failed operations do not create successful-change records. Details missing from older records are not reconstructed. Logs are not an undo tool; make corrections through the relevant editing or cancellation workflow.

# Move guests, exchange units and cancel

## Move a guest to another unit

1. Open the reservation and select Change room.
2. Choose the move operation.
3. Select an available destination room, apartment or bed.
4. Review the guest, dates and destination, then confirm.
5. Verify that the guest appears on the new unit's card.

For checked-in guests, the move takes effect from the operation time. Time spent in the previous unit remains in the history. Do not try to rewrite history by manually changing a unit code.

## Exchange two reservations' units

1. Open Change room for one reservation.
2. Select the exchange operation.
3. Choose the correct second reservation.
4. Review both guests, both units and their dates.
5. Confirm and check both reservations afterward.

Both sides are validated together. Availability or a bed's guest-capacity limit may prevent the exchange. Do not create two new bookings to bypass the error.

## Cancel a reservation before check-in

1. Select the correct reservation.
2. Open Cancel reservation.
3. Complete the confirmation and any required reason.
4. Check the updated reservation status.
5. If money was received, handle any refund separately through [financial transaction records](../finance/transactions.md).

For a guest who has already checked in, use check-out instead of reservation cancellation. Cancellation does not automatically refund money through a bank or payment terminal.

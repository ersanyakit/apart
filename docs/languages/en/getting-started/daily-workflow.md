# A reception shift from start to finish

## Start of shift

1. Sign in with your own account and confirm the selected property.
2. Read urgent and ongoing tasks on the Reception Notes board.
3. Check dirty, out-of-order and renovation units on the Room Status Board.
4. Review today's arrivals and departures in the Stay Calendar and reservation list.
5. Consider late departures together with rooms scheduled for another arrival on the same day.

## During the day

Record new bookings using the [reservation workflow](../operations/reservations.md). Check arriving guests into their existing reservation instead of creating a second booking. Use the reservation's room-change operation when moving guests. Add extra services to the correct reservation folio.

Update the cleaning status when cleaning is complete. Never mark an unprepared room as Clean merely to get past a validation message.

## At departure

1. Open the [pre-check-out folio](../operations/check-in-out.md).
2. Review accommodation and service charges, payments received, refunds and security deposits separately.
3. Complete any required records within your permissions.
4. After confirming check-out, verify the stay and room status.
5. Leave a reception note if the room needs preparation.

## Handover

Clearly record unfinished tasks, expected arrivals and balances requiring follow-up. If you have access to shift and cash reports, compare recorded cash movements with the cash actually counted. Sign out when your work is finished.

# Sign in and explore the interface

## Before you begin

Ask your administrator for the application address, the business you should access and your personal account credentials. There is no shared demo password in this guide. Use the account assigned to you by your business.

## Sign in

1. Open the application address. If you are on the public website, use the sign-in link.
2. Select the hotel you will work with.
3. Select your own staff card.
4. Enter your password and press the sign-in button.
5. Check the hotel and property name on the next screen. If you work at multiple properties, select the correct one before creating records.

If your staff card is missing, first check the selected business. If it is still missing, ask your manager to check your account and property access. Do not use another employee's card to bypass a password error.

## Parts of the interface

- The left menu opens the Room Status Board, Stay Calendar, Reservations and other working pages.
- The hotel and property selector identifies the business context for your work.
- The page header contains search, filters and actions. Expand a collapsed header to reveal hidden controls.
- Notifications at the top right report successful saves or errors. Missing form fields are indicated inside the form.
- On small screens, open the mobile navigation menu to access the same working pages.

After saving, check the success notification and the updated list. If a network error appears, check whether the record was created before submitting the same operation again.

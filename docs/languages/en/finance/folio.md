# Room folio and extra services

Open Folio on a room card, or Room folio / Add service in reservation details.

The folio brings together the reservation's accommodation charges, service charges and payment movements. Read totals separately for each currency.

## Add an extra service

1. Open the correct reservation's folio.
2. Enter or select the service name and category.
3. Specify quantity, unit price and currency.
4. Add any explanation and check the total.
5. Save and confirm the service in the folio movements.

Example: two breakfasts at 150 TRY create a service charge of 300 TRY. This does not mean that 300 TRY has been received from the guest. Record payment separately.

## Correct an incorrect service charge

Use the service cancellation action and provide a reason. Check that the cancellation appears in the folio. Use this action instead of merely changing the description or adding a new service with a negative amount.

Reception staff may add services only to open reservations in properties they can access. Ask your manager if the reservation is closed or outside your access scope.

## Before check-out

Add final services and review payments and refunds. Refresh the folio if another employee has made changes. Acknowledging an outstanding balance during check-out does not erase it.
